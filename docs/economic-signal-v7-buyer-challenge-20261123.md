# V7 buyer/business challenge — acquisition protocol

Frozen 2026-11-23 KST before searching. Separate from earlier GAO challenge (3/20). V7, taxonomy, historical gold and outputs unchanged. No production connector or source authorization is added. Manual public-document evaluation only.

## Fixed sampling

Public direct editorial/customer case-study pages on `shopify.com/blog`, `zapier.com/blog`, and `gov.uk/government/case-studies`. These are published commercial or government case-study accounts, sometimes vendor-selected; reported outcomes are not independent transaction verification. Exclude seller sales pitches without a specific buyer/operating business, sponsored generic advice, security/defense/biological subjects, personal data, gated pages, duplicates. Never bypass access restrictions. Publication date must be explicitly 2022–2025 inclusive. Each arm gets one separate search (up to six results), retain first up to four eligible distinct pages in returned order. Search results only locate pages, not evidence. Do not retry, substitute, or force positive quotas. Retain first short exact excerpt (up to 25 words) relevant to the arm, including counterexamples; not a label. No full raw pages or personal names in retained excerpts. Deduplicate IDs, URL and exact excerpts across arms and observed project corpus. Target 20; report shortfalls. Search-arm labels are kept in receipt only, excluded from inference.
