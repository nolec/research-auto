TED remains the preferred candidate until this feasibility record validates. With both
horizons passing, it may advance to the aggregate-only capacity probe. This decision does not authorize fixture, adapter, or smoke work when that probe cannot satisfy the frozen
coverage quota.

The official Search API provides anonymous access to published procurement notices for
analysis and reuse and explicitly identifies commercial organisations building added-value
services as users. The TED legal notice permits commercial and non-commercial reuse of
published procurement notices unless otherwise stated and dedicates SIMAP metadata to CC0.

The permission boundary is deliberately narrow. Collection excludes contact names, email
addresses, telephone numbers, attachments, unpublished notices, model training, and fine
tuning. The future adapter must request an explicit field allowlist and redact detected
email addresses and telephone numbers from free text before persistence.

Notice identity and evidence independence are separate. A published notice identifier is
the source item identity, while the official procedure identifier is the independence key.
Buyer independence uses allowlisted official organisation identifiers, never contact-point
identifiers or raw contact values.

Passing feasibility promotes TED from preferred candidate to selected replacement source
for a non-persistent capacity probe only. A passing probe is still required before fixture
or real smoke implementation.
