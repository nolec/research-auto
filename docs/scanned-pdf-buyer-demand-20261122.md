## Definition and collection

Search public buyer postings on Upwork and Freelancer for combinations of PDF, Excel, spreadsheet, source verification, discrepancies, scanned, existing spreadsheet, and cross-check. Deduplicate by project URL/ID, not titles; exclude seller service catalogues and bidders' claims. Capture 20 relevant buyer postings, including adjacent jobs. Exact = buyer already has populated spreadsheet, asks to check against scanned PDF/image numeric table. Conditional = populated spreadsheet and source verification specified, but source format or numeric scope unconfirmed. Adjacent = extraction, transcription, or reconciliation bundled with checking, not standalone correction. Explicit manual-only constraints disqualify our AI-assisted offer; financial/personal documents excluded from pilot unless non-sensitive data and permissions established. Listed budgets are not confirmed transaction prices. Upwork search-index descriptions available, direct opens returned 403; live availability unverified. Freelancer status/date shown by opened pages, which may be cached; relative posting ages are as displayed, not independently verified dates.

## Results

- Exact 1/20 (5%); historical completed, not a current lead. Recurring exact-fit 0/20. Conditional 1/20 (5%); source format unknown. Adjacent 18/20 (90%). Even upgrading the conditional listing after source confirmation would leave the original 5/20 gate unmet.
- Four adjacent/conditional Freelancer listings especially worth screening *if* still open and authorized: #2 existing spreadsheet QA (source clarification), #12 same-layout OCR forms (requires extraction), #9 structured PDF batch (scan status unclear), #11 numeric PDF template (financial exclusions apply). #15 similarly open in snapshot but financial and extraction. No currently confirmed exact-fit opportunities.
- Strongest direct historical match is #1: numeric OCR substitution in an already converted spreadsheet. It proves a specific buyer once requested the job, not current recurring demand.
- Adjacent buyer budgets vary widely; explicit low-price bulk jobs and high proposal counts are caution against generic typing. Buyer postings do not establish standalone verification willingness to pay, completed payment, customer acquisition cost or true market size.
